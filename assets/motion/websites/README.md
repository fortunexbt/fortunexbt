# Website motion captures

Actual public websites captured on **8 September 2026**, with an independent Chrome/Playwright browser. These previews show the observed live pages; they are not mockups or a claim that a new release was deployed.

Both websites are shared work with **Walberth Soares** through [Civico Due](https://civicodue.eu/). Keep that credit beside the profile presentation.

- **Bottega Dei Priori:** [public homepage](https://www.bottegapriori.com/), food hero → a short wheel scroll → restaurant photo grid.
- **MUD Escola de Cerâmica:** [Portuguese homepage](https://mudescoladeceramica.com/pt), ceramic-work hero → a short wheel scroll → class overview.

The browser viewport was 1200 × 800 at device scale 1. Each GIF uses a 1.6-second opening hold, approximately 2.6 seconds of observed scroll frames at about 8 fps, and a 1.6-second closing hold. Capture timing was normalized for readable GIF playback. Page content, styles, images, and interactions were not altered; only sizing, frame timing, palette quantization, and GIF compression were applied. No forms, bookings, payments, or contact actions were submitted.

The decoded opening and closing frames were inspected at final size. Both GIFs are under 2,000,000 bytes. [captures.json](./captures.json) records exact source URLs, timestamps, actions, browser version, measurements, hashes, encoding choices, and refresh guidance. Refresh from the public websites after their planned upgrades rather than reusing these as proof of their future state.
